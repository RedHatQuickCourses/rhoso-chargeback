#  Basics of RHOSO Observability

* xref:rhoso-observability-fundamentals.adoc#_introduction_to_rhoso_and_its_purpose[Red Hat OpenShift Service on OpenStack (RHOSO)] Observability provides comprehensive insights into your cloud environment.
* It helps monitor the health, performance, and resource utilization of RHOSO deployments.
* Key features include:
** Monitoring resource usage (CPU, memory, storage)
** Tracking application performance and availability
** Identifying bottlenecks and optimizing resource allocation
** Providing dashboards and alerts for proactive management
* It is crucial for maintaining operational efficiency and ensuring reliable service delivery within RHOSO.
