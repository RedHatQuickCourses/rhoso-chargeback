#  Hands-on Lab: Implementing Your First Chargeback Rating

=== Hands-on Lab: Implementing Your First Chargeback Rating

This lab guides you through the practical steps of configuring and applying your first Chargeback rating. You will learn how to:

* Set up the necessary lab environment for Chargeback.
* Define a basic rating rule for a specific service.
* Apply the newly defined rating rule to your resources.
* Review the generated charges and reports to verify the implementation.
